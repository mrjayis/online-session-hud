the table in the same spot (all players in this session pooled). A line
appears only with 8+ opportunities, at least 12 points off the
table, and a confidence interval clear of it. No money is used.
Citations are master hand numbers; TOO MUCH cites hands where he did
it, TOO LITTLE cites hands where they had the chance and did not.

A player who also appears as a CUT ("-- <game> only") is measured
there against that game's table alone, not against the whole session:
a baseline should come from the table he actually sat at. The two
sections are the same player over different samples, so they can
disagree -- that disagreement is the read.
